01 · the idea

Why a sketchbook?

Divlynx finds the busywork draining a team and replaces it with AI automation. A sketchbook is where that thinking happens: you write down the problem, cross it out, and sketch the fix. Every post should feel like a page from that book.

the three ingredients

1. A black page

Near-black background with a faint dot grid, like a dotted sketchbook page. Never a white background.

2. Clean, confident type

Big, tight headlines in Funnel Display. The type does the serious work and looks professional.

3. Notes by hand in orange

Orange marker strokes, handwritten labels, sticky notes. These add the human, "we thought about this" feel.

the story every post tells

The pain: something done by hand

→ the fix: automated

The value: hours back, no missed leads

the feeling we want

Smart, human, a little playful. Like a senior engineer explaining your problem on a whiteboard. Not corporate. Not "AI robots and blue glow".

03 · typography

Three fonts, three jobs.

All free on Google Fonts (fonts.google.com). Install them in Canva or Figma before designing.

headlines

Funnel Display

Your business runs on autopilot.

SemiBold (600). Big and tight: letter spacing −3 to −4%, line height 95–100%. Max 8 words.

body text

Funnel Sans

We find the tasks draining your team and hand them to AI agents.

Regular (400) and Medium (500). For sentences and explanations. Line height 140–150%.

handwritten notes

Gochi Hand

on autopilot.

Regular, in orange (or dark ink on sticky notes). Only for short notes of 1–5 words: labels, dates, captions, arrows' text.

rules

  • One headline per post. Everything else is clearly smaller.
  • Never write a paragraph in the handwriting font.
  • Never use gradient text, outlines or drop shadows on type.
  • Nothing smaller than 24 px on a 1080 px post. People read on phones.

05 · how much is enough

Sketch with restraint

The hand-drawn layer is the seasoning, not the meal. Too many doodles and it looks cluttered and childish.

do

  • One marker stroke per headline (underline, circle or strike).
  • Max 3 hand-drawn elements per post (e.g. one underline + one note + one arrow).
  • Keep lots of empty black space. Let the headline breathe.
  • Tilt notes and stickers slightly (1–4°). Never more than 5°.
  • Use orange for marks and notes; keep headlines warm white.
  • Put the logo small in a corner on every post.
  • Always follow the story: pain → fix → value.

don't

  • No white or light backgrounds, no other brand colours (blue, purple, green).
  • No gradients on text, no glows around text, no neon "AI" effects.
  • No stock photos of robots, circuit brains or handshakes.
  • No emoji as decoration. Use the hand-drawn marks instead.
  • No paragraphs in handwriting; no more than 2 fonts besides the hand font.
  • Don't stretch, recolour or add effects to the logo.
  • Don't invent numbers. Only use stats we can prove (30+ projects, 20+ clients).

08 · words & post anatomy

How we sound

Short, plain and specific. Name the pain the reader feels, then the value they get. No hype words.

say

  • "Calls after 6pm go to voicemail. Not anymore."
  • "Stop copying leads into your CRM."
  • "Your Monday report, in your inbox at 9am."

avoid

  • "Revolutionary AI-powered synergy solutions"
  • "Unlock the future of work 🚀🤖"
  • Claims or numbers we can't prove

anatomy of a 1080 × 1350 post

1 · hand label

2 · Headline here

3 · One or two lines of body text that explain the value.

4 · one paper object

5 · logo + swipe

Margins: at least 80 px on every side. Keep text out of the bottom 150 px of Reels covers.

09 · before you post

The checklist

  • Black dotted background (#0D0D0C)
  • Headline in Funnel Display, warm white, 8 words or fewer
  • Exactly one marker stroke on the headline
  • Handwriting only for short orange notes
  • 3 hand-drawn elements or fewer
  • Tells the story: pain → fix → value
  • Logo small in a corner
  • Text at least 24 px and readable on a phone
  • Any number or quote is real and approved
